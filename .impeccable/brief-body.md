# Surface brief: the resume (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page resume. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: an IT hiring manager or recruiter in Melbourne, mid
shortlist, with the CV open in another tab and about forty seconds of
attention. They skim, decide the person is real and verifiable, then email,
open LinkedIn or download the CV. Proof is the CV record, the owner's own
verification links and the Poker Money README.

Chosen direction: Rack Elevation. The Nolan descent, the four dream levels,
the dream clock, the film credits, the totem framing and all 3D are retired
at the owner's instruction. Memorable moment: the engineering title block at
monumental scale, carrying the name and the three actions where every
drawing puts it.

Constraints confirmed with the owner: nothing that looks templated, nothing
slow or heavy, nothing that buries the facts, nothing playful or clever. The
IPH title is Service Desk Analyst, as on the offer letter.

Unresolved: real workplace photographs (stock stands in, credited).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The record drawn as the document this trade actually signs off, a
dimensioned elevation with a title block, not a styled CV. Refuses both the
dark terminal portfolio and the sidebar-with-skill-bars resume.

OWN-WORLD: A cool drafting sheet, never cream: sheet #eceef0, ink #10161c,
hairline rule #aab3bb at true 1px. Archivo throughout, already self-hosted:
condensed caps for annotation, plain for reading, tabular figures on every
number. The patch-cable code is the only colour and marks state alone, blue
#1557c0 current, amber #b45309 in progress, red #c6302b escalation, green
#17794a verified. Components are drawn rather than styled: U-numbered rails,
leader lines, callout bubbles, a legend, a revision block.

STORY: The visitor reads the title block, learns who this is and what to do
in one glance, then reads down the elevation: four roles as rack units with
their years as U-numbers, three case files as detail callouts, four
certifications as verified items carrying credential IDs, study and kit as a
schedule, Poker Money as the one detail drawn out. They act from the title
block or the close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed drafting sheet. The title block sits lower right
at monumental scale, carrying MYAT THU in condensed caps, the role, Melbourne,
availability, and three drawn controls with email filled as the primary. The
datum line runs the full width above it, and the first rack unit, IPH
Limited, is already on the rails at the left.

FORM: Rack elevation, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list, assigned by the
roll over my own top-ranked candidate, the runbook. Seed key 2077b2d3.
Raises kept from declined challengers: one identical label schema on every
unit so the sheet scans in a single pass (sneaker archive), every
destination a visible drawn control with no hover-to-discover (HyperCard),
one datum line the whole sheet hangs from (grid horizon), one element owning
the first viewport at full scale (fashion flagship).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
