Case files

Three problems that kept coming back, and what replaced them.

Docks out, hubs in

Retail docks were failing constantly across the estate, and every failure cost a replacement and a callout.

Objective
Stop the stream of failing, costly docks across 400+ stores.
Role
Replaced docks with daisy-chained hub monitors and wrote the rollout playbook.
Outcome
Simpler desks, far fewer failures, and a process anyone could run.

Photograph: Jannis Brandt, Unsplash

Memory, written down

The desk leaned on whoever happened to be rostered, and the same issues kept coming back to the same people.

Objective
Turn tribal knowledge into a consistent, documented process.
Role
Documented procedures, knowledge articles and escalation paths, and became the second-level point.
Outcome
Faster resolutions and a knowledge base new starters pick up quickly.

Photograph: Viktor Talashuk, Unsplash

Day-one access

Builds and access were manual and inconsistent, so no two machines left the bench the same way.

Objective
Secure, standard devices with access ready on day one.
Role
Standard builds, Active Directory and Microsoft 365 administration, joiners and leavers end to end.
Outcome
Compliant endpoints and a tidy account lifecycle.
