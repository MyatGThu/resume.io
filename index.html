The lead

For seven years Myat Thu has worked the part of IT that people only notice when it stops: the laptop that will not enrol, the store that cannot take payment, the account that should have closed on Friday.

He is a Service Desk Analyst at IPH Limited, the second-level escalation point for the desk, building machines to the standard operating environment and working across Active Directory and modern endpoint management.

Before that came retail at national scale. At The Reject Shop he covered more than four hundred stores, cleared forty to fifty tickets on a heavy day, and ran the imaging, onboarding and repairs that kept the estate trading.

The through-line is not the ticket count. It is the habit of turning a repeat problem into a written process that anyone on the desk can run.
